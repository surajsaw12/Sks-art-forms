ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Preserving stories
through art.

This visual reference archive is organised into 10 parts of 20 entries each, following the supplied 200 Indian Art Forms catalogue. Each entry includes its associated region, category, a short introduction, visual points to observe and image-reference links.

Regional associations are not exclusive boundaries. Art traditions may be practised across present-day state borders, and names or spellings may vary by community.
